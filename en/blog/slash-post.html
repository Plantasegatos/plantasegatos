Slash and His Cats: The Feline Side of the Guns N' Roses Guitarist

The Snake Guy (But Not Only)

If there's one image associated with Saul Hudson — the legendary Slash, lead guitarist of Guns N' Roses — it's that of an amateur herpetologist surrounded by giant snakes, top hat on, Les Paul slung over his shoulder. He has been handed reptiles by people who simply didn't know what to do with them, including a six-foot boa constrictor named Pandora, who even starred in the "Patience" music video.

But behind the scales, there's a much fuzzier (and purrier) side that few people know about.

Beatrice: Rock's Most Famous Cat

Beatrice is, without a doubt, the most well-known cat in Slash's life. When she faced cancer and had to go through chemotherapy, it was the guitarist's girlfriend, Meegan Hodges, who shared the journey publicly — asking for positive energy and celebrating every sign of improvement in the cat's test results.

Slash, of course, was right there throughout the treatment. Picture the scene: the man who has shared the stage with giant snakes, sitting beside a recovering kitten. That's rock and roll too.

Angus: Another Name, Another Legend

Another feline who shows up on the family's social media is Angus — a name that, let's be honest, doesn't go unnoticed by AC/DC fans. Coincidence? Maybe. But it's hard not to picture Angus the cat pulling off some feline antics around the house while Angus Young does the same on stage.

The published photos show exactly what you'd expect: a relaxed cat at home, with absolutely no ambition to save rock and roll.

Top Hat, Guitar, and Animal Causes

In 2021, Slash donated an autographed top hat and a Les Paul Special-II guitar pack to a charity auction for Kitten Rescue, an organization dedicated to rescuing kittens. The auction's own promotion couldn't resist a pun, calling the guitarist "one of rock's coolest cats".

In other words: besides having cats at home, Slash also lends his name — and his most iconic symbols — to help kittens who don't have a home yet.

What We Can Take From This

Snakes are for the stage. Cats are for home. And even one of the most iconic guitarists in hard rock history seems to have figured that out — Beatrice and Angus prove that even hard rock legends have their moments of lap time, purring, and belly-up naps on the couch.
