Pacová

Scientific name: Philodendron martianum

☘️ Toxicity: Safe for cats

☀️ Ideal light: Bright indirect light or gentle partial shade

💧 Watering: Moderate — water when the substrate is slightly dry

🌿 Category: Tropical ornamental

The Pacová is a Brazilian tropical plant highly valued in interior design for its bold, glossy leaves and upright growth. Its sculptural appearance and resilience make it ideal for bright corners or decorative pots. Strong, elegant, and safe for cats, it’s a perfect blend of aesthetics and practicality.

Essential care

  • Light: Prefers bright indirect light or gentle partial shade; avoid harsh direct sun on the leaves.
  • Water: Water moderately, allowing the top of the soil to dry slightly between waterings.
  • Substrate: Light, rich in organic matter, and well-draining (mix of potting soil, pine bark, and perlite).
  • Environment: Ideal for indoor spaces with temperatures between 18–28 °C (64–82 °F) and good airflow.
  • Fertilization: Once a month during spring and summer with a balanced diluted fertilizer (NPK 10-10-10).
  • Pruning: Remove old leaves to promote healthy new growth and maintain a compact shape.

Curiosity

💡 Despite belonging to the Philodendron genus, the Pacová is not a climbing plant. It grows upright, forming elegant, compact clumps — perfect for modern and tropical-inspired interiors.

Cat safety

  • Classification: Non-toxic to cats.
  • Best practice: Even though it’s safe, discourage chewing to preserve its shiny foliage.

Quick checklist

  • ☀️ Bright indirect light
  • 💧 Moderate watering
  • 🪴 Light, well-draining substrate
  • 🌿 Mild climate with good airflow
  • 🐾 Safe for cats
