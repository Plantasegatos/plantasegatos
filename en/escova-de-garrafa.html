Bottlebrush

Scientific name: Callistemon citrinus

☘️ Toxicity: Safe for cats

☀️ Ideal light: Full sun

💧 Watering: Low to moderate; tolerates short dry periods once established

The Bottlebrush is a vibrant, woody shrub native to Australia, named after its distinctive red, brush-like flowers. Highly ornamental and easy to grow, it’s a favorite among landscapers for sunny gardens and pollinator-friendly spaces. Hardy, adaptable, and non-toxic to cats, it’s a safe and stunning choice for outdoor areas.

Essential care

  • Light: Requires full sun to produce abundant flowers and dense foliage.
  • Water: Moderate; allow the topsoil to dry between waterings. Once mature, it tolerates short droughts.
  • Soil: Prefers well-draining, slightly sandy soil. A mix of garden soil, sand, and compost works well.
  • Environment: Thrives in warm, ventilated, and sunny areas. Tolerates wind and coastal conditions.
  • Fertilization: Apply organic fertilizer or NPK 10-10-10 every 45 days during spring and summer.
  • Maintenance: Prune lightly after flowering to maintain shape and encourage new blooms.

Pests and diseases

  • Aphids and scale insects: Can appear on young shoots; treat with diluted neem oil.
  • Fungal root rot: Avoid overwatering and ensure good drainage to prevent root damage.
  • Yellow leaves: Usually caused by poor soil or irregular watering — adjust care accordingly.

Propagation

  • Cuttings: Take semi-woody cuttings after flowering and root in light, moist substrate.
  • Seeds: Also possible, but slower. Sow in sandy soil and keep moist until germination.

Cat safety

  • Classification: Non-toxic to cats, according to ASPCA and botanical databases.
  • Interaction: Safe for curious cats; its firm texture and mild scent discourage chewing.
  • Extra benefit: Its flowers attract bees, butterflies, and hummingbirds — enriching the garden ecosystem.

💡 The bottlebrush gets its name from its unique flower shape, which resembles the brushes once used to clean bottles. When crushed, its leaves release a soft lemony scent — a signature trait of the species.

Quick checklist

  • ☀️ Full sun
  • 💧 Moderate watering
  • 🪴 Well-draining soil
  • 🌺 Long-lasting red blooms
  • 🐾 Safe for cats
